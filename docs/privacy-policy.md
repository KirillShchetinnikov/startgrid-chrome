# StartGrid privacy policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026. Draft for publication.

StartGrid displays and manages bookmarks stored in Chrome. It does not send data to developer-operated servers, sell data, or use analytics or advertising trackers.

## Settings and storage

StartGrid stores settings, local background images, bookmark images, usage counters and temporary caches on your device. Chrome manages the bookmark tree and its own bookmark synchronization separately.

When StartGrid synchronization is enabled, Chrome Sync stores selected settings, such as appearance, background URLs, search engines and their URLs, thumbnail behavior, navigation preferences, the home-folder path and keyboard shortcuts. The global selection of which settings to synchronize is itself synchronized and cannot be excluded individually. Your choice of interface language and performance mode, local images, device-specific folder identifiers, usage counters and temporary caches stay local.

Turning off synchronization for an individual setting removes its cloud value when the change reaches Chrome Sync. Each device keeps its own current value. Turning it back on uploads the value from the device where you enabled it. Already released versions that do not support this selection must be updated; they can still upload their old settings format. StartGrid keeps unrecognized settings without interpreting them so compatible versions can coexist.

Turning off all StartGrid synchronization on a device stops that device from exchanging settings but does not delete existing cloud data. The dedicated delete-synchronized-settings action clears StartGrid's Chrome Sync data. Local reset affects the current device and disables its synchronization. You can also export or import settings, clear temporary caches, and remove local images using the extension's controls.

## Optional network features

Features you enable may contact websites directly to retrieve favicons, background images or bookmark thumbnails; Bing for its daily image; and your selected search provider for searches or suggestions. Those services receive the requests needed to provide the feature and apply their own privacy policies. Chrome Sync is provided by Google under its applicable account and synchronization policies.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when StartGrid's data practices change. Questions can be submitted through the [StartGrid issue tracker](https://github.com/KirillShchetinnikov/startgrid-chrome/issues).
